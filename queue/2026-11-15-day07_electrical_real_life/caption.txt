Ever wondered why the kitchen light stays on when a bedroom bulb fuses? 💡

Your home is wired in PARALLEL, so every light has its own path.
Your torch is SERIES: the batteries sit end to end, + to −.

Try this today: switch lights on and off around your home and see which ones work on their own. Then check a torch with two batteries: what happens if one faces the wrong way? (Ask an adult first.)
