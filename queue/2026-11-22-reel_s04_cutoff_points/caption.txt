Your child meets the cut-off point. They can still miss the school. 😮

4 things to know before you choose secondary schools:
1️⃣ The cut-off point (COP) is the score of the last student posted to that school last year, for each Posting Group. A guide, not a pass mark.
2️⃣ It can move every year. MOE says ±1 is typical, and ±2 or 3 is possible.
3️⃣ Same score, one place left? Citizenship first, then choice order, then a computer ballot. So the ORDER of your 6 choices matters.
4️⃣ Use all 6 choices, with at least 2 to 3 schools where your child's score is better than the COP.

PSLE results: Tue 24 or Wed 25 Nov (tentative). You'll have 7 days to submit. Check the latest on moe.gov.sg/s1-posting.
