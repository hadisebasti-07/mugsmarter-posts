"We breathe in oxygen and breathe out carbon dioxide." 🫁 Not quite!

We breathe in AIR. The air we breathe out still has oxygen in it, just less, and it has more carbon dioxide.

In the lungs, some oxygen passes into the blood. The heart pumps it to the body parts, which use it. The blood carries carbon dioxide back to the lungs, and we breathe it out.
