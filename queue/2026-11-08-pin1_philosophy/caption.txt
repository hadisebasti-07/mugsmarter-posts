5 study habits that work for any subject, and how every post on this page puts them into practice:
1️⃣ Understand, don't memorise. Knowing why works on questions you've never seen.
2️⃣ Little and often beats cramming.
3️⃣ Test yourself, don't just reread.
4️⃣ Learn from the mistakes that cost marks again and again.
5️⃣ A head start beats a last-minute rush.

Habits 2 and 3 come from learning research: a 2013 review by Dunlosky and colleagues rated spaced practice and practice testing the two most useful of 10 study techniques.
