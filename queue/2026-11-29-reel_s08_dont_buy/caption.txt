Sec 1 parents, don't buy these yet! 🛒✋

Textbooks, school uniform, PE kit: wait for your child's school.
📋 Each secondary school sends its own book list and uniform details, on or soon after S1 posting day (expected Fri 18 Dec 2026, tentative).
🏫 Many schools sell them through their own vendors, so you get the right items the first time.
⏳ Sale windows can be short (often the last two weeks of December). On MOE financial assistance (FAS)? Check with the school before buying.
