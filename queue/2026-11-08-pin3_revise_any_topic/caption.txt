How to revise any topic, in 5 steps. ✅

1️⃣ Map it: the big ideas on one page, and how they link.
2️⃣ Explain it in your own words, with the book closed.
3️⃣ Test yourself with your notes closed, then check.
4️⃣ Fix your mistakes: write down why each one was wrong.
5️⃣ Come back a few days later, then again a week later.

Steps 3 and 5 are easy to skip, but they're backed by research: a 2013 review by Dunlosky and colleagues rated practice testing and spaced practice the most useful of the 10 study techniques it looked at, and rereading one of the least.

Before your next test, swipe to the last slide and see if you can tick all 5.
