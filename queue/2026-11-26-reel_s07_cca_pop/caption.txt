Which CCA for your kid? Pause and pick A, B, C or D 👀

😆 A. Always moving → Physical Sports (badminton, basketball, football, netball)
😎 B. Loves to lead → Uniformed Groups (NCC, NPCC, Scouts, Red Cross Youth)
🤩 C. Born performer → Visual & Performing Arts (band, choir, dance, drama)
🤔 D. Loves building → Clubs & Societies (robotics, media, debate, chess)

Every Sec school student takes a CCA, from these 4 groups. Each school offers a different list, so check the schools you're choosing on SchoolFinder before Sec 1.

Just for fun, not MOE advice: any kid can try any group, so talk it over with your kid 💬

Each school runs its own Sec 1 CCA selection (usually ranked choices, sometimes trials, and limited places), so a first choice isn't guaranteed.
