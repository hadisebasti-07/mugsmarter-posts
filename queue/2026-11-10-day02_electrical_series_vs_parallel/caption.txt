Series or parallel? Remember it as roads. 🛣️

Series = one road. Block it anywhere and everything stops.
Parallel = a road each. Block one, and the rest keep going.

Swipe for the side-by-side table, and a 2-mark question to try yourself.

⚠️ Skip the "sharing a plate" trick some notes use. It treats current like food that gets eaten up, and that's not how circuits work.
