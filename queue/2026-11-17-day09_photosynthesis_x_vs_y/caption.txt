Photosynthesis or respiration? Remember it as store vs release. 🌱

Photosynthesis STORES energy from light in food. Only green plants, only in light.
Respiration RELEASES energy from food. All living things, all the time.

Yes, plants respire too, day and night. "Plants respire only at night" is one of the most common mistakes.
