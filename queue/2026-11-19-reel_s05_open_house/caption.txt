5 things to check before you choose secondary schools:
1️⃣ The journey. Try the trip on a school-day morning, not just on Saturday.
2️⃣ CCAs. Check that the one your child wants is offered.
3️⃣ Subjects and programmes. Look for what your child enjoys.
4️⃣ Talk to the students. Ask them what a normal school day is like.
5️⃣ Where would your child sit? Look up last year's PSLE score range on SchoolFinder and think about your child's position in the cohort.

Many open houses are on Sat 21 Nov, and some run after PSLE results day. Dates differ by school, so check each school's own website or social media.
