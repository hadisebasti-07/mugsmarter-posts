Where does the energy in your lunch come from? 🍚

Trace it back far enough and you always reach a plant, and then the Sun. Plants use light energy to make food. We eat it, then release the energy by respiration.

Got a fish tank at home? Look closely at the leaves (not the air pump!): in bright light you may see tiny bubbles. They are mostly oxygen from photosynthesis. Turn the lamp off and the bubbles stop, but the plant is still respiring.

Weekend science, no textbook needed. 🌱
