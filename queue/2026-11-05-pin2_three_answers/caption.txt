Half an answer vs a full answer. Can you spot the difference? 🤔

Same question, 3 answers. Our example is a Primary Science question we wrote:
"Mei places a plastic ruler across the gap in her circuit. The bulb does not light up. Explain why."

Answer it out loud first, then swipe.

A repeats the question. B has the right idea but stops halfway. C links every step, from "plastic is an insulator" all the way to the bulb.

The skill works in any subject: whenever a question says "explain why", "give a reason" or "how does … affect …", name the key idea, link each step from cause to effect, and end at what the question asked.
